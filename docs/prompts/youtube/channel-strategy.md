# Channel Strategy Prompts

## 90-Day Roadmap

- **Purpose:** plan a channel's first quarter.
- **Inputs:** `{{niche}}`, `{{budget}}`, `{{hours_per_week}}`.
- **Output:** phased roadmap with checkpoints.

```text
I am starting a YouTube channel about {{niche}} with {{budget}} budget
and {{hours_per_week}} hours/week.

Build a complete 90-day roadmap:
- Weeks 1-2: setup and first videos
- Weeks 3-4: growth tactics
- Month 2: monetization preparation
- Month 3: first income milestone

Include: daily action checklist, weekly subscriber targets, first
monetization date estimate, what to do when growth stalls, and the
biggest mistakes to avoid.
```

## First 10 Videos Blueprint

- **Purpose:** bootstrap a new channel with an ordered launch slate.
- **Inputs:** `{{niche}}`.
- **Output:** ordered list of 10 video briefs.

```text
I am starting a new YouTube channel in {{niche}}.

Give me my first 10 videos:
- Titles optimized for clicks
- Why each video will perform
- Suggested thumbnail angle
- Target audience intent
- Expected views range

Arrange them in the order they should be posted for fastest growth.
```

## Content Calendar

- **Purpose:** generate a 30-day posting plan.
- **Inputs:** `{{niche}}`, `{{videos_per_week}}`.
- **Output:** dated calendar balancing growth and revenue content.

```text
Create a 30-day YouTube content calendar for {{niche}} at a pace of
{{videos_per_week}} videos per week.

Include:
- Video ideas per slot
- Content mix (educational, viral, trending)
- Best posting days
- Which videos target growth vs monetization
- Trending angles to use

Balance the calendar for both views and revenue.
```
