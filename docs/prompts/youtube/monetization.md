# Monetization Prompts

## Income Blueprint

- **Purpose:** map all realistic revenue streams for a channel.
- **Inputs:** `{{niche}}`, `{{subscribers}}`, `{{monthly_views}}`.
- **Output:** ranked income streams with realistic estimates.

```text
I have a YouTube channel about {{niche}} with {{subscribers}} subscribers
and {{monthly_views}} monthly views.

Build a complete income blueprint:
- AdSense revenue estimate
- Top 10 affiliate programs for this niche
- Digital product ideas that fit the audience
- Sponsorship rate card for this channel size
- Merchandise potential
- Paid community model
- Course or coaching opportunity

Show total realistic monthly income at 1K / 5K / 10K / 50K subscribers,
and rank the streams by effort-to-revenue ratio.
```
