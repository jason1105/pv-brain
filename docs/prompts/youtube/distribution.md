# Distribution Prompts

## Algorithm Strategy Checklist

- **Purpose:** derive a distribution strategy and weekly checklist.
- **Inputs:** `{{niche}}`.
- **Output:** channel-level distribution playbook.

```text
Analyze YouTube's current algorithm behavior for {{niche}}.

Tell me:
- Ideal video length for maximum watch time
- Best upload frequency for fast growth
- Tag and metadata strategy that still works
- Thumbnail A/B testing approach
- How suggested-video traffic is triggered
- Community post strategy for engagement
- Shorts strategy that feeds main channel growth
- First-24-hours strategy after each upload

Summarize as a weekly checklist.
```
